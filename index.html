A highly motivated economist with a comprehensive understanding of the application and practical implications of economic theory. My research-based experience in central banking and public-sector consulting spans statistical modelling, time-series forecasting, simulation, and welfare economic analysis.

I have taught economics, mathematics, and statistics to graduate students, and presented research at institutions including the Bank for International Settlements (ARN BIS 2023), SAARC Finance (2022), the University of California Davis, the University of Kansas, the Paris School of Economics, and the Indian Statistical Institute.

I currently serve as a Senior Economist in the Department of Economic and Policy Research at the Reserve Bank of India. I received my doctorate in Applied Mathematics from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne in December 2012, under the supervision of Prof. Bernard Cornet. Earlier roles include Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics and the Big Data Analytics Center at Shiv Nadar University, and Researcher (equivalent to Assistant Professor) in the Management School at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU). I completed my undergraduate degree (B.Math) at the Indian Statistical Institute and an Erasmus Mundus Quantitative Economics master’s jointly from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne and Bielefeld University.
